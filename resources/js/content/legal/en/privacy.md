# Privacy Notice

Mizita, the appointment scheduling platform («Mizita», «we»), is responsible for processing your personal data under Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties, published on 20 March 2025, and its implementing rules.

This notice explains what data we process, why, who we share it with, and how you can control it. It is written to be understood.

## 1. Two different roles, and why that matters

Mizita is a platform where a business runs its appointment book. Two kinds of people therefore meet on it, and our responsibility for their data **is not the same**.

**When you are a business user** — the person who opens an account, or someone on their team — we are the **controller** of your data. We decide what it is used for, and we answer for that to you and to the authority. Everything in this notice applies to you directly.

**When you are an end customer** — someone booking an appointment with a business that uses Mizita — the **controller of your data is that business**, not us. The business decides what it asks you for, what it uses it for, and how long it keeps it. We act as a **processor**: we handle that data only on their instructions, on their behalf, and never for any purpose of our own.

The practical consequence is simple: if you booked an appointment and want to know what is held about you, or ask for it to be deleted, the request goes to the business you booked with. If you write to Mizita we will help route it, but the decision is theirs. Every business is required by our [Terms and Conditions](/terms) to publish its own privacy notice.

## 2. What data we process

### If you are a business user

- **Identification and contact**: name and email address.
- **Credentials**: your password is never stored in the clear; we keep only a derived value from which it cannot be recovered.
- **Google identity**, only if you choose «Continue with Google»: we receive your name, your email, and confirmation that Google has verified it. Of that we store the email, the name, and the identifier Google assigns to your account. We do not ask for or keep your profile picture, and we get no access to your mail or your contacts.
- **Business data**: name, timezone, services, working hours, time off, and team. Some of it is public by design, because it is what a person sees when they come to book.
- **Billing data**, when you subscribe to a paid plan.

### If you are an end customer

- **Name**, and **an email address or a phone number** — either one is enough to book without creating an account.
- **Your appointments** with that business: service, the person providing it, date and time, and status.

Nothing more. We do not ask for an address, a date of birth, or an identity document.

### In every case

- **Technical data** generated by ordinary use of a website: IP address, browser and operating system, date and time of access. It is recorded in the session and in server logs, and serves to operate the service and detect abuse.

## 3. Sensitive data

**We do not collect sensitive personal data** — racial or ethnic origin, health status, genetic information, religious or philosophical beliefs, union membership, political opinions, or sexual preference — and no Mizita form asks for any of it.

An important warning for businesses: an appointment's notes field is free text, and that does not make it an appropriate place to record a diagnosis, a treatment, or any health data about a client. If your work requires clinical records, keep them in a system built for that. A business that enters sensitive data into Mizita does so on its own responsibility, and takes on the heightened obligations the Law imposes on that kind of processing.

## 4. What we use your data for

The Law requires us to separate the purposes that are necessary to provide the service from those that are not. We do that explicitly here.

### Necessary purposes

Without these the service cannot work, and you cannot object to them without ceasing to use it.

- Creating your account, authenticating you, and keeping your session open.
- Running the appointment book: computing available slots from your hours, your time off, and what is already booked, and recording each appointment.
- Sending the emails that are part of the service: booking confirmation, change or cancellation notice, reminder before the appointment, password reset, and email verification.
- Charging for your plan, issuing tax receipts, and keeping accounts.
- Keeping the platform secure: preventing unauthorised access, fraud, and abuse.
- Meeting legal obligations and responding to lawful requests from a competent authority.

### Voluntary purposes

These are not necessary for the service. You can refuse them from the start or change your mind at any time, and refusing affects your account in no way.

- Sending you product news, usage tips, and commercial messages from Mizita.
- Measuring in aggregate how the platform is used, to decide what to improve.
- Inviting you to surveys or to try new features.

To refuse, use the unsubscribe link every message carries, or tell us which purpose you object to. Your refusal takes effect the moment we receive it and needs no justification.

If in future we wanted to process your data for a purpose other than the above, we would tell you and obtain your consent before doing so.

## 5. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. Ever.

To operate we rely on providers who process data **on our behalf and on our instructions** — what the Law calls a remittance, which requires no consent from you:

| Function | What data it reaches |
| --- | --- |
| Hosting and infrastructure | All platform data, at rest |
| Transactional email delivery | Name and email of the recipient |
| Payment processing | Billing data of the business user |
| Error logging and monitoring | Technical data of the failing request |

When a business user **connects their Google Calendar**, and only then, we send their appointment data to Google to keep the two calendars in sync. That connection is switched on deliberately and can be revoked at any time, from Mizita or from the Google account.

Beyond that, we disclose personal data only when required by a competent authority acting within its powers, or where necessary to exercise or defend a right in legal proceedings.

Some of these providers run servers outside Mexico. When we engage them we require contractual guarantees that they will handle data in line with this notice and at a level of protection equivalent to the one Mexican law imposes.

## 6. Bookings without an account, and the manage link

You can book on Mizita without creating an account. In that case we send a link that lets you view, move, or cancel that booking.

**That link is a credential.** Whoever holds it can see and change the booking, exactly as the person who made it can. Treat it the way you would treat a password: do not forward it or post it anywhere. It expires some time after the appointment.

If you later create an account with the same email address, **we only join your earlier bookings to that account after you confirm the address** by clicking the message we send. That is a deliberate precaution: without it, anyone registering an account by guessing an email address would see that person's appointment history across every business on the platform.

## 7. How long we keep data

- **Your account and business data**: for as long as the account is active.
- **Appointments**: for as long as the business keeps them. They are its record of activity, and the business decides.
- **Billing data**: for the period Mexican tax law requires, even if you close your account.
- **Technical and security logs**: a short period, enough to investigate an incident.

When you delete a record or close your account, we first mark it as deleted — it becomes inaccessible and disappears from the application immediately — and we erase it permanently from our live systems within {{RETENTION_DAYS}} days. That interval exists so an accidental deletion can be reversed and any open billing dispute can be closed. Backups are overwritten on their own, slightly longer cycle, and a deleted record is never restored from them except to rebuild the whole system after an incident.

## 8. Your ARCO rights

You may ask us at any time for:

- **Access** — to know what data of yours we hold and what we use it for.
- **Rectification** — to correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete.
- **Cancellation** — to stop processing it and delete it, where no legal obligation requires us to keep it.
- **Objection** — to stop using it for a specific purpose, on legitimate grounds.

You may also **withdraw your consent** for any voluntary purpose, and **limit the use or disclosure** of your data.

To exercise these, send us your request from the email address linked to your account, and include:

1. Your name and a way to reply to you.
2. A copy of official identification, or of the authority of anyone acting for you.
3. Which right you are exercising and over what data.
4. For a rectification, the corrected value and documents supporting it.

We will reply within a maximum of **twenty working days** and, where the request is well founded, give effect to it within the following **fifteen working days**. The procedure is free; we could only pass on shipping or reproduction costs if you ask for the information on physical media.

If you believe we did not handle your request properly, you may turn to the **Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno**, which has been the competent data protection authority for private parties since the 2025 law came into force.

## 9. Security

We encrypt traffic between your browser and our servers, store passwords using a derivation function built to resist brute force, and limit data access to the people who need it to run the service.

Each business is isolated from every other by design: a person's membership of a business's team is the only thing that grants access to its data, and that check runs on every request.

No system is invulnerable. Should a security breach occur that significantly affects your rights, we will tell you without delay, what data was affected, and what you can do.

## 10. Minors

Mizita is intended for adults. We do not create accounts for anyone under 18. A business may serve customers who are minors; where that happens, the business is responsible for obtaining consent from whoever holds parental authority or guardianship.

## 11. Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies to keep your session and protect forms. We use no advertising or third-party tracking cookies. The detail is in the [Cookie Policy](/cookies).
