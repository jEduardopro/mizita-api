## 1. What Mizita is, and what it is not

Mizita is a scheduling tool: it lets a business publish its services and its hours, and lets its customers book an appointment.

**Mizita does not provide the services that get booked.** When someone books a haircut, a consultation, or a session, the contract is between that person and the business. We provide the calendar; we neither deliver the service, nor supervise it, nor answer for it.

## 2. Definitions

- **Staff** — each person who works at a business and appears in its book. Adding someone as staff is also what grants them access to that business's data, with whatever role they are given.
- **End customer** — whoever books an appointment with a business, with or without a Mizita account.
- **Service** — something a business offers, with its duration, its buffers before and after, and its price.
- **Booking** — a confirmed appointment, which occupies its duration plus the configured buffers in the calendar.
- **Platform** — the Mizita website, its API, and any applications we publish.
